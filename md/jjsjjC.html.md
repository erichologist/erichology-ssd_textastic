[^12][^18]   - *example* (HTML):```lang-html<h1>My Awesome Article</h1><h2>Introduction</h2><p>This is the intro text.</p><h2>Main Section</h2><p>Content here.</p><h3>Sub-Section</h3><p>More details.</p><h4>Sub-Sub-Section with <strong>important words</strong></h4>```### ***What it Would Target***: - The `<strong>` in "Section 1.1.1" (if structured with headings). - It won't target `<strong>` if it's directly in the <h1> because h1 isn't a child of another element here.### ***Purpose***:[^12][^19]It helps style specific, structurally significant bolding within your content for better readability and user experience, following logical heading hierarchy (`H1 > H2 > H3` etc.).[^11]:https://blogpros.com/blog/2019/07/should-tags-blog-posts[^12]:https://www.hobo-web.co.uk/headings-seo-checklist/[^13]:https://fantinel.dev/blog/quantity-queries/[^14]:https://www.freecodecamp.org/news/difference-between-b-and-strong-in-html/[^15]:https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/Heading_Elements[^16]:https://www.dummies.com/article/technology/programming-web-design/general-programming-web-design/html-css-javascript-all-in-one-for-dummies-cheat-sheet-299575/[^17]:https://alvaromontoro.com/blog/68082/style-headings[^18]:https://www.mediavine.com/heading-tag-seo/[^19]:https://semji.com/blog/why-use-h1-h2-h3-tags-for-your-seo/
