blending whatsoever. ||   `multiply`  | the element is multiplied by the background and replaces the background color.<br>The resulting color is always as dark as the background.   ||   `screen`    | multiplies the background and the content then complements the result.<br>This will result in content which is brighter than the background-color. ||   `overlay`   | multiplies or screens the
