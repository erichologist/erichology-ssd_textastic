You can declare this in the `<head>` section of your HTML:```lang-html<meta charset="UTF-8">```### Syntax:- Decimal format: `&#` followed by the decimal number and a semicolon (e.g., `&#57332;`).- Hexadecimal format: `&#x` followed by the hexadecimal number and a semicolon (e.g., `&#xE2C4;`).
