"frosted glass" look (requires a background with opacity, like bg-white/70).hidden md:block: This is a Responsive Modifier. It hides the desktop links by default and only shows them on "medium" screens (768px) and wider.items-center & justify-between: The "Dynamic Duo" of Flexbox. It puts the logo on the left and the links on the right automatically.Pro Tip: Hover StatesNotice the hover:text-indigo-600 and hover:bg-indigo-500. Tailwind allows you to style any state (hover, focus, active) just by adding a prefix.What should we build next?A Hero Section (the big "Welcome" part of a site)?A Pricing Table?A Footer?<!--  -->
