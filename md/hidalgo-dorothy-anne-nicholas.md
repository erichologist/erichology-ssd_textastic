Mrs. Hidalgo passed away after a prolonged and courageous battle with cancer. She was pre-deceased by her husband Alexander, a member of a prominent New York Real Estate family. Mrs. Hidalgo was born in Salisbury, NC, the daughter of Mr. and Mrs. Moses Nicholas. She received her education in Salisbury and moved to New York City to pursue a career in fashion and advertising. She was Editorial Director for Vogue - Butterick and later Vice President - Account Executive at Grey Advertising. She joined her husband in the real estate business and, as Director, was responsible for the company's expansion in to property management. Mrs. Hidalgo received many awards for her civic activities. She served as President of the English Speaking Union of New York for 12 years and as an active Board Member of the Visiting Nurse Association of New York she chaired many successful programs designed to help the less fortunate. Donations to either of these organizations can be made in Mrs. Hidalgo's name. Mrs. Hidalgo is survived by her son Neal A. Hidalgo, her daughter Melene Nahodil and her husband Robert, her grandson Alexander Nahodil, and her grand-daughter Alesandra Nahodil. At the request of Mrs. Hidalgo a private, family only, service will be held at a later date.
