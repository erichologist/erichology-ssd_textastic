The mix-blend-mode property accepts several values, including (but not limited to):

normal:
The default value; no blending occurs.
multiply:
The colors of the element and its backdrop are multiplied, typically resulting in a darker color. White becomes transparent, and black remains black.
screen:
Multiplies the complements of the colors, resulting in a lighter color. Black becomes transparent, and white remains white.
